***Some of them have premium upgrades but they all let you use them for free.

1. Elsa Speak
Website: elsaspeak.com

ELSA uses AI to help you improve your pronunciation. With its speech recognition technology, it provides instant feedback and suggests corrections. 


2. Memrise
Website: memrise.com
It offers interactive lessons focusing on vocabulary and pronunciation. It uses spaced repetition to help you retain words effectively.

Best For: Vocabulary building and speaking practice.
Unique Feature: Real-life videos for better context understanding.
3. YouGlish
Website: youglish.com
YouGlish uses AI to help you learn the correct pronunciation of English words. 

Search for a word, and it shows video clips where native speakers use it in real-world contexts.

Best For: Pronunciation and context learning.
Unique Feature: Video-based pronunciation examples.
